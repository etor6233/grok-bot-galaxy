# Why Grok Bot

Source: clip 04 slide “Why Grok Bot” (~00:04:35) plus Roman’s spoken product decisions.

Three decisions that shaped the product (spoken):

1. **Teammate paradigm, not task paradigm.** Feels like iMessage / colleagues. Sidebar is bots with jobs, not threads per task. You come back to the same bot; it learns (example: slide-formatting preference remembered weeks later).
2. **Bots have a computer**, not only APIs/MCPs. They can click through software a human would use (including old tools), watch video, listen to audio. Integrations help but often stop at ~90%; computer use is how work finishes end to end.
3. **Everything runs in the cloud** (always-on). Not tied to your laptop. Automations still fire at 2am if the lid is closed; you can kick work off from a phone.
