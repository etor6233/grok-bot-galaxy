# AI Maturity Curve

Source: Grok Bot 101, clip 04 ~00:00:00, on-stage slide. Presenters: Roman (speaking), Amrita (on stage).

Grok Bot is positioned at the top of a four-step curve, not as a chatbot.

| Stage | Label on slide | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ask (Chatbots) | You prompt; it answers |
| 2 | Do a task (Copilots) | It helps complete a discrete task |
| 3 | Delegate outcome (Bot) | You hand off an outcome; a bot owns it |
| 4 | Staff function (Team of Bots) | A team of bots runs a function (GTM, eng, admin, …) |

Product implication (spoken in the same module): you do **not** open a new chat per task. You create a **bot for a job**, return to it, and it keeps memory.
