# Delegate founder work to a persistent bot team

Source: Shubh's Day 1 founder workshop, 2026-09-15, [056](../../timelines/056.md)–[061](../../timelines/061.md). These are presenter workflows and demo examples, not guaranteed business outcomes.

## Start with the work

Inventory your recurring work, group it by domain, and give each bot a clear responsibility, relevant tools, and a way to verify its output. Shubh recommended spending one or two hours on that inventory and asking for **execution plus verification**. He preferred direct specialist conversations; a chief of staff was another valid arrangement. [056, 05:10–08:20](../../timelines/056.md); [059, 00:00–02:30 and 06:35–09:59](../../timelines/059.md)

## Demonstrated roles

| Role | Inputs → output | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Close Bot | Calendar, customer telemetry, website → call brief with screenshots, usage graphs, recommendations, risks | [057, 00:00–03:30](../../timelines/057.md) |
| Close Bot follow-up | Call transcripts → better future briefs; customer feedback → prototype requests | [057, 03:30–08:25](../../timelines/057.md); [059, 00:00–02:30](../../timelines/059.md) |
| Prod Bot | PRs, issues, live product walkthrough, metrics → shipped/unshipped changes, QA evidence, decisions needed | [057, 08:25–09:59](../../timelines/057.md); [058, 00:00–01:10](../../timelines/058.md) |
| Stalk Bot | Competitor products, changelogs, public posts, hiring → periodic teardown and relevant changes | [058, 01:10–05:15](../../timelines/058.md) |
| Proto Bot | Selected feedback plus its own test account → prototype and QA with cloud agents | [058, 05:15–07:40](../../timelines/058.md) |
| Yap Bot | Past writing plus edits to drafts → style context used by other bots | [058, 07:40–09:59](../../timelines/058.md) |
| Misc Bot | Unrelated one-off questions → answers without filling specialist conversations | [058, 07:40–09:59](../../timelines/058.md) |

Close Bot's Northwind customer data was explicitly **example data**. Its on-screen credit draft required a **send instruction** and a **separate yes to apply credits**, despite the broader narration about autonomous campaigns. Preserve the actual permission boundary when adapting the pattern. [057, 00:00–08:25](../../timelines/057.md)

## Build the feedback loop

1. Give the bot the task, the relevant systems, and the result you can inspect.
2. Review what worked and what did not; retain the useful role-specific learning. Close Bot used Granola transcripts for this; Yap Bot learned from draft-versus-sent differences.
3. Route context between specialists when relevant: customer feedback to Proto Bot; product changes back to Close Bot.
4. Convert recurring work into an appropriate schedule or incoming signal, with quiet runs when there is nothing relevant.

Sources: [057, 03:30–08:25](../../timelines/057.md); [058, 01:10–09:59](../../timelines/058.md); [059, 00:00–05:15](../../timelines/059.md).

## Review remains part of delegation

The founder still chooses which feedback to pursue. Product walkthrough videos and screenshots make that review concrete. Treat competitor hiring as a signal to investigate, not proof of a roadmap. Shubh described account signups and churn outreach as part of his workflow; those examples do not grant a bot blanket authority to create accounts or contact people.
