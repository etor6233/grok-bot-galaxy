# Define an ICP and prepare an outreach workflow

Evidence: Day 3, 2026-09-17. Matthew Silberman's **Cerebro** demo produced an ideal customer profile (ICP) and draft outreach angles. Clay and Amplemarket were not connected during the demonstrated company-search flow.

1. Give the GTM bot a product brief. In the demo, Cerebro asked **Chief** to explain the game before locking its output.
2. Distinguish users from buyers. Thursday Arena's players were one audience; potential advertisers and partners were another.
3. Describe the user's situation, not only industry or company size. The team's working hypothesis was a short brain break between meetings, which suggested adjacent habits such as news, snacks and casual games. It was a hypothesis, not measured audience research.
4. Ask for fit, anti-fit, personas, prioritized segments, qualification criteria and signals. Signals should explain why a particular company is relevant, rather than merely finding a contact.
5. Source the proof for any pitch. Cerebro asked other bots for verified traction and retained the date and measurement caveats.
6. If the required tools are connected, use the proposed sequence: find companies, identify relevant people, prepare personalized sequences, and monitor replies. Distinguish a plan from an executed connector query or sent message.

| Output | Demonstrated example |
|---|---|
| Product context | Short auto-battles; proposed stadium advertising |
| Player hypothesis | Work-break casuals, scroll substitutes, bot-curious visitors |
| Buyer segments | News/briefings, snacks/drinks, casual games |
| Fit signals | Relevant sponsorship/paid acquisition activity, suitable product and audience |
| Draft angle | Verified launch traction plus why the placement fits the buyer |
| Proposed tool sequence | Clay company search → people → Amplemarket draft sequence → inbox monitoring |

The template described a **Find My ICP** skill, but the installed bot reported that it had no such skill. It used an equivalent reasoning flow, disclosed the gap, and waited for product context. Later, it correctly declined to invent Clay search results because Clay had not been installed. Do not treat a template description as proof that its skills, accounts or connectors are ready.

The strongest traction block explicitly separated signed-in players, total matches and matches in flight. It warned against claiming peak concurrency, counting matches as unique humans, or describing async ghost matches as live PvP. Keep that evidence discipline in every draft.
