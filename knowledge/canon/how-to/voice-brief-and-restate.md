# Voice-dump a brief, then force a restatement

Source: Engineering clip 14 (Lauren → Steve).

1. Turn on the microphone and talk for a few minutes (faster than typing while presenting).
2. End with: **restate to me what I just said in your own words so I know you understood me.**
3. Use the restatement as the spec; pass that framing to specialist bots (here: Steve → grokpot).

Do not start from a generic “make a landing page” — include the actual offer (e.g. French fries / operators-first waitlist).
