# Research and scope a business before automating it

Source: Day 1 company build and guest advice, 2026-09-15. These are recorded choices and guest suggestions; they do not establish that the demo business made sales or became a finished platform.

## Research a specific outcome

Pick the immediate business result before creating a bot. The hosts chose lead capture and potential paying customers; their Operator Research bot had four research-only tracks: local operators, useful websites, comparable pop-ups, and similar companies. Its brief required **cited shortlists, no invented contacts, no cold outreach**. [020, 03:56–09:00](../../timelines/020.md)

Ground every new bot in a short company document: current goal, owners, audience, decisions, and open questions. The demo chief of staff made reading the Notion founding document a rule for new bots. [020, 07:28–09:00](../../timelines/020.md)

Codie's distribution suggestions included a useful lead magnet based on distinctive data and a proof vault tracking testimonials, verification, and permission to share. These were advice, not product features or guaranteed acquisition results. [020, 01:23–03:56](../../timelines/020.md); [021, 01:15–03:35](../../timelines/021.md)

## Do the work, then choose what to build

1. Identify the smallest real workflow you need personally. The hosts decided to run their own pop-up and build tools for actual pain they encountered. [029, 00:00–02:00](../../timelines/029.md)
2. Ask the bot to restate your brief before executing. When venue research became the priority, steve proposed pausing the event-management prototypes; Lauren confirmed. [043, 00:00–01:10](../../timelines/043.md)
3. Give parallel agents different bounded tasks. The venue effort split map/search/calling and contact lookup into separate cloud-agent tasks. [043, 03:20–06:20](../../timelines/043.md)
4. Bring human examples back into the brief. Matt used known venues as comparison points and added capacity and space-control criteria. [045, 03:35–08:00](../../timelines/045.md)
5. Validate the result in use, then generalize. The hosts repeatedly narrowed scope; the broader platform became a stretch goal. By the Day 1 close, even the event concept was still under review. [054, 02:36–04:08](../../timelines/054.md); [064, 00:00–01:00](../../timelines/064.md)

## Separate proposals from shipped behavior

Merch generation, programmable plushies, collectible tickets, and event-platform ideas were explored on stage. They were not newly announced Grok Bot capabilities. Record an idea, a prototype, a reviewed PR, and a deployed result as different states. The demonstrated verification flow exposed missing videos, failed media uploads, and review blockers before shipping. [054](../../timelines/054.md); [046, 05:00–09:30](../../timelines/046.md)
