# How to teach a task (skills)

Source: clip 05 ~00:05:03–00:06:36. Bot: **Slide Sonya**.

1. Open the bot. Control **its** computer (you drive, it is not autonomous in this step).
2. Hit **Teach a task**.
3. Perform the work once (demo: Insert → animation → fly-in, play to verify).
4. **Stop the task**.
5. The recording becomes a **skill**. Later you can say “add that animation I showed you” to text or an image.

Also: put standing instructions in the bot **description** (font, colors; always screenshot the finished slide and send it back).
