# Hire bots as employees

Source: Engineering 10–18.

- Treat bots as hires. **Funny names**, not generic role labels (chat named the research bot **Marky McMarkface**).
- One job per specialist. Talk to a **chief of staff** (Lauren renamed default → **steve**) and have it route.
- **Dr. Eggbot** / CreateAgent: factory installer — spawn specialists (tater = cracked engineer), install Cursor team kit, skills, p-stack, cloud-agent hookup.
- Optional: “status every 3 minutes”; nudge or interrupt.
- **Don’t dump unbounded context.** One short company doc, shared to every bot.
- Templates are shareable. Healthchecks from Eggbot were saved **paused**. Coding bar spoken: **poteto-mode** (one job, unslopped, verified).
