# How bots talk to each other

Source: clip 06 ~00:01:47–00:05:19.

Two patterns:

1. **Standing delegation in the description.** Example: a Chief of Staff that does nothing itself and always delegates to specialist bots.
2. **Explicit mention in the thread.** UI can show `message_data_dan`. Demo: tell Email Ethan to message Slide Sonya for details on the Grok Bot Galaxy 101 deck.

What you see: an inter-bot thread. Ethan: “Amrita asked me to…”. Dan: still working on the spreadsheet, use sample coffee data. Sonia: scan the full deck and pass Ethan an outline.

Amrita’s name for this: **agent-to-agent protocol / communication**. You manage orchestration; bots are long-lived personas with memory (Ethan could run a year and keep every email; Sonia’s job is slides in general, not one deck).

Orchestrate by **project** or by **expertise** — your choice; “you’re creating your own company / team of agents.”
