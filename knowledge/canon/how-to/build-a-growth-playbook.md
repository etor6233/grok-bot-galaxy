# Capture and prioritize a growth playbook

Evidence: Day 3, 2026-09-17. Vincent Zhu and Roshan used a **Vincent** bot and Notion to turn a live product walkthrough into proposed experiments.

1. Walk the entire user journey. They examined X sign-in, the leaderboard, a match, the win screen and anonymous practice.
2. Dictate ideas as they arise and ask the bot to record them in a shared playbook. A rough transcript was enough to start the idea log; the humans refined it through discussion.
3. Store each idea with **channel, impact, effort and status**. The displayed statuses were Backlog, Next, Shipped and Killed. Add a short deep dive for ideas selected for the next step.
4. Establish the baseline metrics and goal before ranking ideas. Vincent's priorities assumed that the team wanted more X sign-ins.
5. Select a few experiments, pass their scope to engineering, and review results. “Next” means prioritized, not implemented.

The three priorities in the session were a soft X sign-in prompt after two or three practice runs, outreach using Lauren's established audience, and sharing a win or leaderboard result. Other ideas included direct challenges, rarity rewards, welcome DMs and streak rewards. These were brainstormed plays; the footage does not establish that all were shipped or that any had improved conversion.

The win-screen sharing idea subsequently became a cloud-agent task with an Open Graph card. That is evidence of a build handoff, separate from an experiment result.

An adapted brief:

> Keep a living growth playbook while we review this product journey. For each idea, record the user moment, intended action, channel, hypothesis, impact, effort and status. Separate suggestions from shipped work. Rank a small next batch against our current funnel goal, and preserve unresolved decisions for review.

Vincent emphasized product-market fit and warned against surrounding users with too many asks. The reusable lesson is to improve an identified part of the experience, then measure it. His general distinction was marketing for awareness and growth for what happens once people enter the product.
