# Run sales workflows with specialist bots

> Dated addition — Day 2, 2026-09-16. The sales demonstration uses fictional accounts and planted call notes. This guide describes demonstrated workflows, not current connector availability or guaranteed sales outcomes.

Give each bot a job, the account context, and access to the tools that hold the evidence. The demo used **Olive** for daily preparation, **PG** for prospecting, **Echo** for call notes and decks, **Customer Expert** for account context, and **Engineer** for technical questions. [087 · 00:03:56–00:05:35](../../timelines/087.md).

## Prepare, prospect, and follow through

1. **Prepare the day.** Ask Olive to prioritize meetings, email, and Slack. Review its draft email card, edit if needed, and use the send/discard controls. The presenter limited inbox routines to once or twice daily to reduce noise and consumption. [087 · 00:05:35–00:06:53](../../timelines/087.md).
2. **Select accounts.** Give PG an explicit account list or a Salesforce source and a bounded research batch. The presenter described a five-account, five-contact workflow. Request ranked contacts, personal hooks, intent signals, and their supporting sources in a sheet. [087 · 00:07:23–00:09:59](../../timelines/087.md).
3. **Draft in your voice.** Provide examples from your authorized email, Slack, or X context. Ask PG to tie the message to what the recipient actually said or did. The visible prospecting output was labeled demo data and draft-only. [087 · 00:08:44–00:09:59](../../timelines/087.md), [088 · 00:00:00–00:01:19](../../timelines/088.md).
4. **Turn a call into next steps.** Give Echo the meeting notes or recording reference and the target deck. It refreshes the customer-specific slides; Salesforce next steps were drafted in a defined format and reviewed before being pushed. For Granola, the Q&A described stopping the recording before running Echo. Do not infer continuous live transcription from the workshop's broad “live call” framing. [087 · 00:06:53–00:07:23](../../timelines/087.md), [088 · 00:00:00–00:01:19](../../timelines/088.md), [090 · 00:01:17–00:02:39](../../timelines/090.md).
5. **Keep the account plan current.** Ask Customer Expert to maintain a Notion account plan from calls, stakeholders, usage, renewal context, and feature requests. The demo linked newly shipped features to earlier customer requests and used Slack for actionable notifications. [088 · 00:01:19–00:02:39](../../timelines/088.md).
6. **Route technical uncertainty.** Ask Engineer for an implementation-grounded answer, then review the customer wording. The separate [sales-engineering guide](run-sales-engineering.md) explains the repository-backed workflow. [088 · 00:02:39–00:03:59](../../timelines/088.md).

## Reuse the setup

Share role-based templates, then personalize them for the rep, account book, tone, and tools. Connect the organization's existing knowledge sources; the Q&A cited Notion, Slack, Salesforce, and Databricks. Specialist bots and one bot per strategic customer were preferences, not mandatory product architecture. [089 · 00:01:14–00:06:15](../../timelines/089.md), [090 · 00:00:00–00:01:17](../../timelines/090.md).

Before accepting output, verify the account and recipient, the evidence behind the hook, the call/deck version, and whether the result is a draft or a completed write. **Forecast** appeared in the use-case list and sidebar, but these clips do not establish a reproducible forecasting implementation.
