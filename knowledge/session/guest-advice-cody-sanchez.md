# Guest playbook: Cody Sanchez (clips 019–021)

Source: Engineering block, remote guest Cody Sanchez (@Codie_Sanchez), 2026-09-15. Session layer — guest advice for the pop-up, not product canon.

## Who

Business-buying/selling marketplace + advisory that helps people scale businesses. She showed two live tools: a Vercel dashboard for a book launch (~135,355 users, real-time attendees, paid vs organic, CAC vs AOV) and **Pulse**, their business-scoring dashboard ("put together a plan based on your lowest score… profit levers").

## Her playbook for the team

1. **Validation before building:** sell it to three people before you decide to build. A restaurant pop-up isn't a VC pitch (~2/2,000 companies get VC); cash flow funds it.
2. **Distribution is the moat** (Jack Dorsey agrees, per her X DMs): products keep getting easier to build; the #1 reason AI investments fail is no distribution. Build in public and keep a **proof vault** — every good thing said about the product, categorized (testimonials, views, screenshots, verified/unverified, shareable or not).
3. **Lead magnets, not bare signups:** use unique data (e.g. survey the AI-interested audience), give more value than the email costs ("value switch"), post on X, spin 5–6 magnets ~5 minutes each. Her example: 74-question survey over 15,000 small-business owners → 12 owner archetypes (built with Claude + Vercel + Replit).
4. **Controversy with a CTA:** the Austin-move AI video (Higgsfield, ~1.5 h editor time) → 2.4 M views → hundreds of applicants; the hyperbolic "don't do ads until 5–10 M" take → ~5.7 M views → ~2,000 book signups. Make a company X account; lead with "what do you believe that others don't."
5. **Ads later:** no ads before you know the persona and what organic works; then put dollars behind proven organic.
6. **Management:** "hire A players and get out of their way" almost bankrupted her 150-person company — the working version is clear asks + incentive alignment + intense oversight (track cash-on-cash, AOV, LTV against hires' goals).
7. **Research bot spec (built live):** top SF restaurateurs → 5 best websites → 5 best pop-up pages → 5 best pop-ups in any city (6–12 months) → one document; then "how would Danny Meyer position a pop-up" style prompts; landing page = one-line hook + exclusivity/scarcity/timeliness/relevance.
8. **Grok Bot recipe:** connect TweetHunter to X → feed top-performing tweets → "pull me the top 15, steal the frame, not the exact verbiage."
9. **Parting rule:** business exists to profit; "if you build it, they will not come."
