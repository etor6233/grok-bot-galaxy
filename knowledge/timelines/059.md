## 00:02:30–00:05:15  [slide]
**Spoken:** Shubh — bonus tips. (1) **Browser use is super powerful but expensive to run multiple times** — "bias towards APIs and interfaces people already built — or hack it: ask your bot to look at network requests while it does a task once, figure out which APIs are being hit, and then hit those directly. Way less time, way more token-efficient — no more browser interface. And for ALL optimizations: always just ask your bot how to do it; it will figure it out." (2) **Audit your routines**: "The frequency data on routines freaks me out. The most important part is to be intentional about how often they run — most people over-index. 'This is so important, I need it every 15 minutes' — that's 100 times a day, very expensive. Try running them fewer times — or run them on an inbound signal that lets the bot take an input and then run, rather than blindly running on a schedule. Those two things alone will save a bunch in tokens." (3) **Make a voice bot**: "Please do this for yourself — the most underrated way to get words out to other people: a bot that copies your voice." (4) **Import your cookies**: "Lets you stay signed in across tools and stops you from constantly taking over the bot's computer to help out."
**On screen:** Tips slides (APIs over browser use, routine audits, voice bot, cookies).
**Actions:** Shubh gives four power-user tips, centered on token economy.
**Facts:**
- Token tip: watch network requests once → call those APIs directly instead of repeated browser use
- Routine audit: avoid every-15-min routines (100×/day); prefer fewer runs or inbound-signal triggers
- Voice bot = underrated way to get words out; import cookies to keep bots signed in and reduce human takeover
