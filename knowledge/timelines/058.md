## 00:07:40–00:09:59  [talking-head]
**Spoken:** Shubh — two final bots. **YapBot**: "One purpose only: it exists to talk like me. It looks through my emails, Slack, iMessage, any communication I've done, and learns how I talk. It doesn't learn once — it compounds: every week or whatever schedule you set, it takes everything I've sent and gets better. Now I can trust it to talk as me — and the other bots proactively bring YapBot in to talk like me across surfaces; after a few times they learn to loop in the other bots as needed. It seems small but it's so important for outbound and fast comms. For sensitive emails you might ask it to keep drafts instead of sending — and even then it learns from the delta between the draft and what was actually sent, getting better over time.
