And I think that's really what differentiates Grok Bot and what makes it a true, like, force multiplier for SDRs. So I really emphasize like thinking at a high level — kind of thinking how you can actually build that system. And I think on that same note, you should be **intentional** here. So it's really intuitive to just prompt each bot. And what I highly recommend is just taking a step back and thinking, 'okay, let me build this all through my actual chief of staff.' Bots should be able to talk to each other. They should have that context. The point of this is to offload more work from you and help you actually get higher outputs. And so you shouldn't actually be focusing even harder and messaging even more bots. It's exhausting from just all the prompt and context switching you're doing there. And lastly, what I kind of hope I really showed in my demo is how to think **systematically** about your setup. I think it is very easy to just always want to make new bots. And it's honestly more chaotic, and it does more harm than good. And so you should really be questioning, like, 'why is it important that a new bot does this? But really, I emphasize thinking of all these different things as ways for you to actually just save more tokens in the first place, but actually also just not confuse yourself and not kind of just get overloaded with bots that are just doing like kind of similar things.
