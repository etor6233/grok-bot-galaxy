## 00:07:40–00:08:27  [ui-demo]
**Spoken:** Presenter: what's really cool is you can choose how much human you want to bring into the loop. For a really easy change — say a documentation website, or resizing something in the mobile app — it's low stakes and we can let our agents run with it. One thing we've found generally true: giving agents an environment to verify their own output makes a huge difference, and giving them the tools to help you (the human) verify the output also makes a huge difference in outcomes.
