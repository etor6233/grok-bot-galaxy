**Spoken:** After the extraction gap, Amrita describes an always-on force multiplier for distributed teams. Public Marketplace templates are linked from QR coasters; teams also share bots to use consistent resources. Amrita (why Grok Bot): "Main things: it's very easy to use — a lot of tools, when you first spin them up, you don't know where to start or how to connect things. Grok Bot learns all of that for you — you'll see it when we build a bot from scratch today: it asks me 'what am I being used for and how can I best help you?' And we'll do something cool: we'll ask one of the Grok Bots to **spin up its own team of bots** — 'what bots do you think would be helpful for you? If I asked you to hire your own team, what would be useful?' — and a bot can actually make that decision. Bots spinning up other bots, bots working with teammates — that's what makes Grok Bot this multiplayer force-multiplier tool. And computer use: it can do an entire task end to end. What I really like: if I give it a task — it's in the cloud, always on — I can come back and trust the task will be done. It's not going to sit waiting for my approval, not going back and forth on decisions — it finishes the task, and I can tweak or steer it however I want. The goal: you're not sitting and waiting — you're coming back to **finished work**, which really saves a lot of time."
