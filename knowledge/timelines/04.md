## 00:01:26–00:02:31  [slide]

**Spoken:** Roman: Second difference — like a colleague, Grok Bot does not only have direct integrations, MCPs, and APIs. Those are useful but often do not get you 100% of the way. Bots have access to their own computer and can use it exactly as you would: watch a video, listen to a podcast, click through antiquated government software from the 90s. Everything a human can do with a computer, but it is yours. Integrations and MCPs plus computer use open a new set of tasks AI can take off your plate. It feels different when AI can do those things versus cannot. Difference between getting 90% of the way (almost done) versus the job done end to end, hands off, you trust the bot — feels magical.
**On screen:** Same “Introducing Grok Bot” UI. Computer card remains “Working” / Sign in to Salesforce. Callout “Log bots into your tools and they use them like you.”
**Actions:** Roman keeps walking the screenshot; highlights computer-use, not only APIs.
**Facts:**
- Bots have a computer, not only integrations / MCPs / APIs
- Computer use can watch video, listen to audio, and drive arbitrary software (including old tools)
- Integrations often stop around ~90%; computer use is how work finishes end to end
- Whisper “MCIs” = MCPs

## 00:02:31–00:03:33  [slide]

**Spoken:** Roman: Last thing — everything runs in the cloud, not on your computer. Weird moment in AI: many workflows people rely on run on local laptops. If you are asleep and the computer is closed, the automation set to run at 2 a.m. does not fire; you wake confused; you want to kick something off from your phone but it is tethered to the laptop in the office. They wanted to jump past that: smart colleagues / teammates that live in the cloud, interact anywhere you work — phone, laptop, or something different in the future. Those three decisions informed the product: feels like teammates; everything runs in the cloud; bots have a computer they can drive like you.
**On screen:** Same Introducing Grok Bot UI through ~00:03:16, then product screenshot stays while he recaps the three decisions.
**Actions:** Roman recaps the three product decisions on the still UI.
**Facts:**
- Everything runs in the cloud, not on the user’s laptop
- Automations still fire if the lid is closed (example: 2 a.m.)
- You can kick work off from a phone; not tethered to one machine
- Three product decisions: teammate feel, cloud/always-on, bot-owned computer
