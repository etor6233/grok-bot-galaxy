## 00:03:49–00:05:43  [slide]
**Spoken:** David: "One powerful thing: if there's a missing piece, you can likely have Grok Bot build it. ## 00:05:43–00:08:31  [slide]
**Spoken:** David: "Second: alerting. At scale you have many tickets across different customer states, and it's hard to find what needs attention right now. Grok Bot can alert. Anyone on your team can spin up a Grok Bot connected to your ticketing system and tell it: create a routine that looks through tickets every hour, classifies if a user is threatening to churn and has been a customer a certain time, then sends an alert to a Slack channel so everyone has visibility. Third: an internal Q&A agent. Other support agents focus on customer replies; it's surprisingly cumbersome to use your existing knowledge base to answer teammates. Query it intelligently within Slack. Fourth: it can improve itself — give it your traces or have it look back on the last week of tickets to find improvements (handled better, flagged earlier), and it can go do those things."
