## 00:00:00–00:05:00  [talking-head]
**Spoken:** (continues from 060) Shubh Q&A. Marketplace trust: "At least for our marketplace you can probably trust the bots in there — they've gone through a pretty thorough review, which includes human review, and also **we have bots that review the bots**, which is pretty fun." Q (cross-account bot talk): "How do bots talk to each other across people and accounts? It's something we're working on — trying to see the best form factor. It does not exist in our capabilities today, but it's a very cool use case and we're excited for it." Q (local execution): "How do we use local and the bot's computer at the same time? Go to your settings and turn on **local execution** — allow it by default if you want. It lets you send messages from your computer directly, and drive your browser. The reason we bias towards moving work to the bot's computer is we think that's the future — as models get better and computers stronger, you delegate off your device. Doing things locally is kind of janky: apps pop up on your screen while you work (no parallelism), and it uses your computer's resources — annoying for high-intensity stuff. But you can use it right now: just go to settings." Q (shared memory): "Do all bots share the same memory? They do not share the same memory, but they do share the same file system. A bot's computer is actually one VM with a bunch of different instances — like different desktops on your computer — and they share files; they will proactively read each other's files for a task. Their context windows are not the exact same." Q (Cursor interplay): "We have a first-class integration — a perk of Grok and Cursor being together: you can kick off cloud agents and Grok Bot transfers only the relevant context to them; they work independently and come back with results. Then Grok Bot — as long as it has access to the environment — can review and QA it, test it, even look at the PR and work with it directly. The important part is figuring out when to use a cloud agent vs Grok Bot: complicated tasks, shipping with full control over which model, wanting to go in and change things → Cursor cloud agent. Trying it a few times gives you the intuition; spin up a few and get them to ship things — it does a surprisingly good job with the orchestration handoff."
