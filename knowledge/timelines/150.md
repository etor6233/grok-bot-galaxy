## 00:05:43–00:08:15  [talking-head]
**Spoken:** Demo wrap — what we learned running a full campaign build start-to-end. First: scope your bots properly — identifying a bot/job is like crafting a job description (a new teammate with swim lanes and roles, tightly scoped so they're specialized and get that specific work done; that's how you squeeze the most efficiency out of your team of bots). Second: trust your bots — these teammates are ambitious, proactive, hungry; give them the access they need (hook them into Slack, email; say "go study all the context and history and tell me what you can take off my plate"), throw ambitious tasks at them. Third: invest in your bots — they get better over time; bot-to-bot collaboration gets more organic as you prompt it, and they improve as you feed feedback, context, memory (compounded value, like a teammate).
**On screen:** Summary card: "What we learned — Scope Bots properly; Then, trust your Bots!; Invest in your Bots and copy others'."
**Actions:** Presenter delivers the three-lesson wrap-up to camera.
**Facts:**
- Lesson 1 — scope bots tightly (like a job description: swim lanes, roles, specialization)
- Lesson 2 — trust bots: give access (Slack/email) and ambitious tasks
- Lesson 3 — invest in bots: bot-to-bot collaboration + memory/feedback compound their value over time
