## 00:05:10–00:08:20  [slide]
**Spoken:** Shubh (why founders): "You have three key execution pieces that make or break your company: (1) preserve your focus — a trillion things hit you all day; (2) maintain velocity across everything your team ships; (3) protect your ability to make great, high-quality insights — good decisions and planning. Grok Bot makes all three easier. Most importantly, it can handle all the distraction-actions in your day-to-day — the stuff that is necessary but not the most important — you just hand it off. That mental load it saves you is probably more important than anything else I'll talk about. Second: get tasks done end to end — prompt your bots to do ALL the work, including execution and verification. Third: triage the firehose — there's a constant flow of information (my screen time on X is embarrassing) and to keep insight quality high you need to triage it; bots are well positioned for that." Best time to build: "Lingxi, one of our engineers, mentioned earlier he ships way faster with Grok Bot than ever before — kicking off cloud agents, delegating full tasks… **Routines** take care of the pieces you'd otherwise think about constantly — schedules, webhooks, signals — set it once and it happens forever. And finally — most importantly on this slide — you can do things that don't scale, except you can actually scale them. We've all heard how most startups win their first customer: some hacky, niche, super-janky prototype, they sign, you breathe a sigh of relief — then you do it 100 times again. With Grok Bot you can make all of that happen without the constant headache. Founders are the most high-agency people out there — a product that enables that agency gets very cool ships in the next few years."
