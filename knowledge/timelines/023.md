## 00:04:35–00:07:24  [slide]
**Spoken:** Lingxi Li — "Why Grok Bot" (four reasons). You can literally work anywhere — for example, lie on the grass at Golden Gate Park while your bot is working." On any platform — iOS, Android, or iPad — you can directly click through the computer to get things done, whether it's logging into services that are hard to navigate or require some sort of human verification — or when it's doing some login but you don't want to hand your password away, you can use the mobile client or desktop clients to click through the remote computer." (4) **First-party coding agent integration**: "This only exists at Cursor. Our Cursor cloud agent integration with Grok Bot is very, very smooth — almost everything you can do in the user interface of Cursor is possible with Grok Bot."
