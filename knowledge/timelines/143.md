Then the agent can take action on the user's behalf. That saves a human's time. Maybe perhaps there's an Escalate Issue tool… for your restaurant, you call in, the voice agent delivers the hours, and the user says 'can you help me place an order?' — you could have an Escalate tool that forwards that call to the [human].
