So using the VM can be a little bit more costly. And then routines can be expensive because people over-index on how often they need them… it's really easy to have something check every 15 minutes all the time because you can just ignore it if you don't want it. But those things sitting in the background add up. If you have three of those that run 15 minutes all the time, that's hundreds of messages coming a day… I also think, like, keep your team lean. You don't need 45 bots… keep your team really specialized in what they need, and that really helps as well."
**On screen:** Talking-heads on stage with the audience.
**Actions:** Blake fields the cost and integrations question.
**Facts:**
- Integrations: connect other software/systems via MCP APIs, configurable directly on the bot's VM
- Cost guidance: built cost-efficient, but be strategic — full-UI VM runs (e.g. building a form by clicking) cost more than API-style paths (e.g. Google Forms AI); routines are the hidden cost (three 15-min routines ≈ hundreds of messages/day); keep the team lean and specialized (no 45-bot orgs)
