## 00:05:00–00:08:40  [ui-demo]
**Spoken:** Roshan (game design): "Our initial plan: make it easy to take a template and convert it into a character — and what better way than building a **team of bots**. Maybe we give some stats to these bots, taking inspiration from our favorite games: **Charisma, Dex, Intelligence** — three stats for each bot. I've always been a fan of abilities too — maybe each bot has an ability that lets it use one of these stats (a charisma ability, dex ability, or int ability)." Lauren: "Give the ability a cool name — 'charisma ability' feels too techy. Shameless plug: one of my favorite bots is Dr. Eggbot — I'd imagine it has a cool skill like 'unslop' or one of the skills I made. It adds flavor, and under the hood it uses one of those stats." Roshan: "Maybe you start a bot and draft your team around it — upload a template, we generate a character with some randomness, you get an avatar with some visuals — and to keep it simple you have your main bot plus a few additional drafted bots: a team of three to start." Lauren: "Imagine: I sign up, add Dr. Eggbot to my team, plus Chadbot and the Outbound Prospecting bot — how do we determine their stats?" Roshan: "We probably want it **deterministic** — upload a bot, generate a character, same stats every time; mint a bot into a character and it persists. And a **total stat pool**: games like this work well with a fixed pool — say 100 max, split into the three stats. We could put an LLM against the bot's description: a bot described as charismatic/coordinating gets a charisma bonus; a bot good at connecting to many services gets high dexterity. Still fixed — the three stats total 100." Lauren: "Minimum of 1, max 98 — no zero stats. It could just be random."
**On screen:** Whiteboard with the stat model: three stats, total pool 100, min 1 / max 98, ability-per-stat, team of 3.
**Actions:** Panel designs the stat system (CHA/DEX/INT pool of 100, LLM-scored from the bot description, deterministic minting).
**Facts:**
- Character stats: Charisma, Dex, Intelligence — total pool 100 (min 1, max 98 per stat), deterministic per bot
- Stats scored by an LLM reading the bot's description (charismatic/coordinating → CHA bonus; many-connections → DEX)
- Abilities: one per character, tied to a stat, with flavor names (Dr. Eggbot example: "unslop")
- Team size: main bot + 2 drafted = 3-bot team; avatars generated with randomness
