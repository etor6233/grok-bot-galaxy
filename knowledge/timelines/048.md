## 00:07:55–00:08:40  [slide]
**Spoken:** Presenter: to that point, we've seen a lot of folks create many agents, so let me spend a minute on why that's effective. One: while agents might have massive context windows that can store entire novels, our brains — at least my brain — can't. So sometimes we just want to know who to reference, and it feels like a natural pattern to assign roles that correspond to how you'd interact with colleagues. Having a person who's in charge of data — maybe they do the job of five data people, or assist with five data-type tasks — is really helpful for invoking and working with those agents knowing their role. I find it helpful just to know which bot I want to talk to for a given task. And then sometimes their roles merge, and that's okay.
